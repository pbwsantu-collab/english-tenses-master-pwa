172. What is Tense?

The Tense is the change of form in a verb to express the time of an action.

ক্রিয়ার যে পরিবর্তন দ্বারা কাজের সময় বোঝায় তাকে Tense বলে।

Three principal tenses: Present • Past • Future

Each has four forms: Indefinite, Continuous, Perfect, Perfect Continuous

174. Present Indefinite (Simple Present)

S + V1 (s/es)  |  do/does + not + V1

  • Universal truth — The earth is round. (নিত্য সত্য)
  • Habit — He works hard. (অভ্যাস)
  • Future (context) — The school closes on the 15th.
  • Historic Present — Alexander now rushes upon the enemy.

Note: 3rd person singular takes s/es.

175. Present Continuous

am / is / are + V-ing

Action going on now or near-future arrangement.

কাজটি বর্তমানে চলিতেছে।

⚠️ Not with: believe, know, like, love, want, belong, consist…

176. Present Perfect

has / have + V3

Just finished • Past action with present result • Period not finished.

এইমাত্র শেষ বা ফল এখনও চলছে।

⚠️ Cannot take yesterday, last night, ago…

177. Present Perfect Continuous

has/have + been + V-ing

for = period  |  since = point of time

পূর্বে শুরু হয়ে এখনও চলছে।

178–181. Past Tenses

Past Indefinite: V2 / did + V1 — I saw him yesterday.

Past Continuous: was/were + V-ing — He was reading when I came.

Past Perfect: had + V3 — He had left before I came. (Before before, after after)

Past Perfect Continuous: had been + V-ing

অধিকতর অতীত ক্রিয়াটি বুঝাইতে Past Perfect।

182–184. Future Tenses

Future Indefinite: shall/will + V1

Future Continuous: shall/will + be + V-ing

Future Perfect: shall/will + have + V3

Future Perfect Continuous is rarely used.

185–189. Strong, Weak & Confusing Verbs

Strong: change inside vowel (sing–sang–sung)

Weak: add -d/-t (work–worked)

Zero-change: cut, put, hit, cost, shut…

Lie (rest) lie–lay–lain  |  Lie (false) lie–lied–lied  |  Lay (put) lay–laid–laid

Hang (suspend) hung  |  Hang (execute) hanged

Fly flew–flown  |  Flee fled  |  Flow flowed

Present Tenses

Present Indefinite

I/You/We/They write  •  He/She/It writes

নিত্য সত্য, অভ্যাস, স্থায়ী অবস্থা, নির্দিষ্ট ভবিষ্যৎ।

Present Continuous

I am writing  •  He is writing  •  They are writing

এখন চলমান কাজ বা আসন্ন পরিকল্পনা।

Present Perfect

I/You/We/They have written  •  He/She has written

এইমাত্র শেষ; বর্তমান ফল; সময় এখনও শেষ হয়নি।

Present Perfect Continuous

has/have been writing

অতীতে শুরু হয়ে এখনও চলছে। for / since

Past Tenses

Past Indefinite

I wrote  •  He wrote  •  did + V1 (negative/question)

অতীতের একক কাজ বা অভ্যাস।

Past Continuous

was/were + V-ing

অতীতের কোনো মুহূর্তে চলমান কাজ।

Past Perfect

had + V3

অন্য অতীত কাজের আগে সম্পন্ন। Before before, after after.

Past Perfect Continuous

had been + V-ing

অতীতের কোনো সময় পর্যন্ত চলমান কাজ।

Future Tenses

Future Indefinite

shall/will + V1

ভবিষ্যতে ঘটবে এমন কাজ।

Future Continuous

shall/will + be + V-ing

ভবিষ্যতের কোনো সময়ে চলমান কাজ।

Future Perfect

shall/will + have + V3

ভবিষ্যতের কোনো সময়ের আগে সম্পন্ন কাজ।

Strong & Weak Verbs

Strong Verbs

Change inside vowel; no -d/-t in Past. sing–sang–sung • go–went–gone • write–wrote–written • take–took–taken

Weak Verbs

Add -d or -t. work–worked • play–played • bend–bent • bleed–bled

Zero-change (189)

bet, burst, cast, cost, cut, hit, hurt, let, put, rid, set, shed, shut, split, spread, thrust

Confusing Verbs

Lie / Lay

Lie (rest) lie – lay – lain (intransitive)

Lie (false) lie – lied – lied

Lay (put) lay – laid – laid (transitive)

থাকা → lain | মিথ্যা → lied | রাখা → laid

Hang

Suspend → hung  |  Execute → hanged

Fly / Flee / Flow

fly → flew – flown  |  flee → fled  |  flow → flowed

Born / Borne

bear (birth) → born  |  bear (carry) → borne

Strike / Stroke

strike → struck / stricken  |  stroke → stroked
