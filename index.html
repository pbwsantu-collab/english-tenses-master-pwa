Complete Tense Rules

সম্পূর্ণ নিয়ম • বাংলা ব্যাখ্যাসহ (Art. 172–190)

172. What is Tense?

The Tense is the change of form in a verb to express the time of an action.

ক্রিয়ার যে পরিবর্তন দ্বারা কাজের সময় বোঝায় তাকে Tense বলে।

Three principal tenses: Present • Past • Future

Each has four forms: Indefinite, Continuous, Perfect, Perfect Continuous

174. Present Indefinite (Simple Present)

S + V1 (s/es)  |  do/does + not + V1

  • Universal / general truth — The earth is round. Honey is sweet. (নিত্য সত্য)
  • Habitual or permanent action — He works hard. This watch keeps good time. (অভ্যাস)
  • Future action (when context shows futurity) — The school closes on the 15th. He will go home when you come.
  • Historic / Graphic Present — Alexander now rushes upon the enemy. (অতীত ঘটনাকে উজ্জ্বল করিয়া প্রকাশ)
  • Present action — It rains. How he walks. (More often Present Continuous is preferred)
  • Quotations from authors — Goldsmith says, “Handsome is that handsome does.”
  • Present Perfect sense — I hear (= have heard) he is ill. I am told (= have been told)…

Note: 3rd person singular → add s / es. All other cases → base form.

175. Present Continuous

am / is / are + V-ing

Action going on at present or near-future arrangement.

কাজটি বর্তমানে চলিতেছে বা আসন্ন ভবিষ্যৎ।

⚠️ Not normally used with state verbs of perception, feeling, knowing, possession:

believe, feel, hear, hope, know, like, love, see, understand, want, wish, wonder, belong, consist, contain, matter, possess, resemble…

Modern usage accepts: am feeling, is looking.

Verbs ending in -e drop e before -ing: come → coming, rise → rising.

176. Present Perfect

has / have + V3 (Past Participle)

  • Action just finished — I have done my duty. The sun has set.
  • Past action whose results still continue — I have done a great wrong (I am still suffering).
  • Action in a period of time not yet finished — I have been laborious from my boyhood.
  • Future Perfect after when / before / after / as soon as — I shall go after I have finished the work.

⚠️ Cannot be used with yesterday, last night, ago, last week…

With some verbs of motion: He is gone / The sun is set (be + V3).

177. Present Perfect Continuous

has / have + been + V-ing

Action started in the past and is still continuing.

ক্রিয়াটি পূর্বে আরম্ভ হইয়া এখনও চলিতেছে।

for = period of time  |  since = point of time

I have been doing this for six months. It has been raining since Monday.

178–181. Past Tenses

Past Indefinite: V2 / did + V1 — single act, habitual past, or polite form.

Past Continuous: was / were + V-ing — action going on in the past.

Past Perfect: had + V3 — action completed before another past action.
Memory aid: “Before before, after after”

Past Perfect Continuous: had been + V-ing — action continuing up to a past point.

অধিকতর অতীত ক্রিয়াটি বুঝাইতে Past Perfect ব্যবহার হয়।

182–184. Future Tenses

Future Indefinite: shall / will + V1

Future Continuous: shall / will + be + V-ing

Future Perfect: shall / will + have + V3 — action completed before a future point.

Future Perfect Continuous is rarely used and is omitted in modern practice.

Modern tendency often prefers Simple Future even where Future Perfect is traditionally required.

Present Tenses

চারটি রূপ • বাংলা ব্যাখ্যাসহ

Present Indefinite

I/You/We/They write • He/She/It writes

নিত্য সত্য, অভ্যাস, স্থায়ী অবস্থা, নির্দিষ্ট ভবিষ্যৎ, Historic Present, quotations.

Examples: The earth is round. He works hard. The school closes on the 15th. Alexander now rushes upon the enemy.

Present Continuous

I am writing • He is writing • They are writing

এখন চলমান কাজ বা আসন্ন পরিকল্পনা।

State verbs (believe, know, like, want, belong…) সাধারণত এই tense-এ হয় না।

Present Perfect

I/You/We/They have written • He/She has written

এইমাত্র শেষ; বর্তমান ফল; সময় এখনও শেষ হয়নি।

yesterday / last night / ago দিয়ে ব্যবহার করা যায় না।

Present Perfect Continuous

has / have been + V-ing

অতীতে শুরু হয়ে এখনও চলছে। for = period, since = point.

Past Tenses

চারটি রূপ • বাংলা ব্যাখ্যাসহ

Past Indefinite (Simple Past)

I wrote • He wrote • did + V1 (negative/question)

অতীতের একক কাজ, অভ্যাস, বা বিনয়সূচক ব্যবহার।

I did it. He studied hard. Could you please tell me the time?

Past Continuous

was / were + V-ing

অতীতের কোনো মুহূর্তে চলমান কাজ।

He was reading when I saw him. They were playing football.

Past Perfect

had + V3

অন্য অতীত কাজের আগে সম্পন্ন।

“Before before, after after”

He had left before I came. He left after I had gone.

Past Perfect Continuous

had been + V-ing

অতীতের কোনো সময় পর্যন্ত চলমান কাজ।

I had been working when he came. He had been suffering from fever before he came here.

Future Tenses

চারটি রূপ • বাংলা ব্যাখ্যাসহ

Future Indefinite

shall / will + V1

ভবিষ্যতে ঘটবে এমন কাজ।

I shall do it. He will go home.

shall → 1st person; will → 2nd & 3rd person (traditional rule)

Future Continuous

shall / will + be + V-ing

ভবিষ্যতের কোনো সময়ে চলমান কাজ।

I shall be sleeping at that time. He will be doing it.

Future Perfect

shall / will + have + V3

ভবিষ্যতের কোনো সময়ের আগে সম্পন্ন কাজ।

He will have done this before you come.

Modern usage often prefers Simple Future in these contexts.

Future Perfect Continuous

Rarely used in modern English and therefore omitted from practical teaching.

Strong & Weak Verbs

Art. 185–189 • বাংলা ব্যাখ্যাসহ

Strong Verbs

Form Past Tense by changing an inside vowel (no -d / -t suffix).

কেবল ভিতরের vowel পরিবর্তন করিয়া Past হয়।

sing – sang – sung • drive – drove – driven • write – wrote – written • take – took – taken • go – went – gone

Weak Verbs

Form Past Tense by adding -d or -t (with or without vowel change).

শেষে d বা t যোগ করিয়া Past হয়।

work – worked • play – played • bend – bent • bleed – bled • teach – taught • seek – sought

Zero-change Verbs (Art. 189)

Same form in Present, Past and Past Participle:

bet, burst, cast, cost, cut, hit, hurt, let, put, rid, set, shed, shut, split, spread, thrust

Important Notes

  • Some weak verbs look strong because they change the vowel (teach–taught, seek–sought) but they are weak because they add -t.
  • Past Participles in italics are mainly used as adjectives: bended knees, drunken man, molten lead, sunken eyes, stricken people.
  • Modern preference: burnt, dreamt, dwelt, knelt, leapt, smelt, spelt (rather than burned, dreamed…).

Confusing Verbs

Art. 188 • বিভ্রান্তিকর ক্রিয়া

Lie / Lay

Lie (rest) — intransitive → lie – lay – lain
থাকা — It has lain here. He has lain in bed.

Lie (false) — intransitive → lie – lied – lied
মিথ্যা বলা

Lay (put) — transitive → lay – laid – laid
রাখা — Lay it here. He has laid the foundation. The hen has laid eggs.

Hang

Suspend → hung – hung  |  Execute → hanged – hanged

He hung the coat. The murderer was hanged.

Fly / Flee / Flow

fly → flew – flown (birds, planes)

flee → fled – fled (escape)

flow → flowed – flowed (river, time)

⚠️ The river has overflowed (not overflown). The bird has flown (not fled).

Born / Borne

bear (give birth) → born  |  bear (carry) → borne

He was born of poor parents. She has borne two sons. He was borne on the shoulder.

Strike / Stroke

strike → struck / stricken  |  stroke → stroked

He struck me. The people were awe-stricken. She stroked the cat.

Lose / Loose / Loss

lose (verb) – cease to have
loose (adj./verb) – free / make free
loss (noun) – that which is lost

Awake / Wake

awake → awoke / awaked • wake → woke / waked

Usage varies; both forms are accepted in modern English.
