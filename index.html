Complete Tense Rules

সম্পূর্ণ নিয়ম • বাংলা ব্যাখ্যাসহ (Art. 172–190)

172. What is Tense?

The Tense is the change of form in a verb to express the time of an action.

ক্রিয়ার যে পরিবর্তন দ্বারা কাজের সময় বোঝায় তাকে Tense বলে।

Three principal tenses:

  • (a) Present Tense – action in the present time: I go. He writes. They are playing.
  • (b) Past Tense – action in the past time: He wrote. They were playing.
  • (c) Future Tense – action in the future time: I shall go. He will write. They will be playing.

173. Each principal tense has four forms: Indefinite, Continuous, Perfect, Perfect Continuous.

174. Present Indefinite (Simple Present)

S + V1 (s/es)  |  do/does + not + V1

Used to refer to:

  • (a) Universal or general truth (নিত্য সত্য বুঝাইতে): The earth is round. Honey is sweet. Honesty is the best policy.
  • (b) Habitual or permanent action (অভ্যাস বুঝাইতে): He works hard. This watch keeps good time. He takes bath in the Ganga everyday.
  • (c) Future action when futurity is indicated by context (নির্দিষ্ট ভবিষ্যৎ কালের ক্রিয়া বুঝাইতে): He will go home when you come. The school closes on the 15th.
    Note: Common with verbs of motion – come, go, start, return, etc.
  • (d) Past events to make narrative more vivid (অতীত ঘটনাকে উজ্জ্বল করিয়া প্রকাশ করিতে) – Historic or Graphic Present: Alexander now rushes upon the enemy.
  • (e) A present action (বর্তমান ক্রিয়া বুঝাইতে): It rains. How he walks.
    But more often Present Continuous is used: It is raining. He is walking.
  • (f) Quotations from authors (কোন গ্রন্থকারের লেখা উদ্ধৃত হইয়াছে বুঝাইতে): Goldsmith says, “Handsome is that handsome does.”
  • (g) Present Perfect sense (এইমাত্র কাজটি হইল বুঝাইতে): I hear (= have heard) he is ill. I am told (= have been told) that he will come.

Note: (a) 3rd person singular → add s or es. (b) All other cases → base form of the verb.

175. Present Continuous

am / is / are + V-ing

Expresses:

  • (a)(i) An action going on at present (কাজটি বর্তমানে চলিতেছে বুঝাইতে): I am writing. He is sleeping.
  • (ii) A future action (আসন্ন ভবিষ্যৎ কালের ক্রিয়া বুঝাইতে): I am going there tomorrow.

⚠️ Not generally used with verbs of perception, feeling, emotion, knowing, thinking, etc.:

believe, feel, hear, hope, know, like, love, see, understand, want, wish, wonder, belong, consist, contain, matter, possess, resemble…

Examples: I feel (not am feeling) unwell. I hope for the best. I want a pen. It matters little.

But am feeling, is looking are now used even by good writers.

Structure notes:

  • Verb takes -ing.
  • am → I; is → 3rd person singular; are → all others.
  • Verbs ending in -e drop e: come → coming, rise → rising.

176. Present Perfect

has / have + V3 (Past Participle)

Used:

  • (a) To express an action just finished (কাজটি এইমাত্র শেষ হইয়াছে বুঝাইতে): I have done my duty. The sun has set.
  • (b) To express a past action whose results still continue (কাজটি পূর্বে শেষ হইলেও ফল এখনও চলিতেছে বুঝাইতে): I have done a great wrong. He has been laborious.
  • (c) To express an action done in a space of time not yet fully gone (কাজের সময়টি এখনও চলিতেছে বুঝাইতে): I have been laborious from my boyhood.
  • (d) To express Future Perfect after when, before, after, as soon as, etc.: I shall go there after (or when) I have finished the work.

⚠️ Notes:

  • Some intransitive verbs of motion/change form Present Perfect with be: He is gone. The sun is set. The moon is risen.
  • Cannot be qualified by past-time adverb/phrase: He died (not has died) last night. I came (not have come) here yesterday.
  • has → 3rd person singular; have → all others.

177. Present Perfect Continuous

has / have + been + V-ing

Expresses that the action has been going on for some time and is not finished yet.

ক্রিয়াটি পূর্বে আরম্ভ হইয়া এখনও চলিতেছে বুঝাইতে।

I have been doing this for six months. It has been raining since Monday last.

for = period of time  |  since = point of time in the past.

has been → 3rd person singular; have been → all others.

178. Past Indefinite (Simple Past)

V2  |  did + V1 (negative / question)

Used to denote:

  • (a) A single act in the past (অতীত ক্রিয়া বুঝাইতে): I did it.
  • (b) An action actually going on in the past: “While they danced (= were dancing) we sang (= were singing).”
  • (c) An habitual action in the past (অতীত অভ্যাস বুঝাইতে): He studied (= used to study) hard. He held his class here.

Also sometimes used:

  • For an action begun a short time before and perhaps continued up to the time of speaking: I came (= have come) to ask you to vote for Mr. Dutta.
  • As a polite or deprecatory form: Could (= can) you please tell me the time? Was (= is) this the sort of thing you wanted?

179. Past Continuous

was / were + V-ing

Denotes that an action was going on in the past (কাজটি অতীত কালে চলিতেছিল বুঝাইতে).

He was reading when I saw him. They were playing football.

was → singular subjects (1st & 3rd person); were → all other cases.

180. Past Perfect

had + V3

Expresses an action complete before another past action (কোন অতীত ক্রিয়ার পূর্বে আর একটি ক্রিয়া সম্পন্ন হইলে অধিকতর অতীত ক্রিয়াটি বুঝাইতে).

He had left before I came. The train had started before I reached the station.

Memory aid: “Before before, after after”

He had come before I did. He left after I had gone away.

Modern tendency sometimes uses Past Indefinite even for Past Perfect.

181. Past Perfect Continuous

had been + V-ing

Denotes that an action had been going on at or before some point of time in the past (একটি অতীত কালের পূর্ব হইতে আর একটি কাজ চলিতেছিল এই অর্থে অধিকতর অতীত কালটি বুঝাইতে).

I had been working when he came. He had been suffering from fever before he came here.

Form is the same for singular and plural in all persons.

182. Future Indefinite

shall / will + V1

Denotes an action that will happen in the future (ভবিষ্যতে কোন কাজ হইবে বুঝাইতে).

I shall do it. He will go home.

Traditional rule: shall → 1st person (I, we); will → 2nd & 3rd persons.

183. Future Continuous

shall / will + be + V-ing

Represents an action as going on at some future time (ভবিষ্যতে কোন কাজ চলিতে থাকিবে বুঝাইতে).

I shall be sleeping at that time. He will be doing it.

184. Future Perfect

shall / will + have + V3

Denotes that an action will be completed at some point of time in the future (ভবিষ্যতে দুটি কাজের মধ্যে অধিকতর অতীত কালটি বুঝাইতে).

He will have done this before you come (= will come).

Modern use often prefers Simple Future: I shall go there before you come. They will finish the work before you reach.

N.B. Future Perfect Continuous is no longer in practical use.

Present Tenses

চারটি রূপ • সম্পূর্ণ নিয়ম ও উদাহরণ

Present Indefinite

I/You/We/They write • He/She/It writes

নিত্য সত্য, অভ্যাস, স্থায়ী অবস্থা, নির্দিষ্ট ভবিষ্যৎ, Historic Present, quotations, Present Perfect sense.

Present Continuous

I am writing • He is writing • They are writing

এখন চলমান কাজ বা আসন্ন পরিকল্পনা। State verbs সাধারণত হয় না।

Present Perfect

I/You/We/They have written • He/She has written

এইমাত্র শেষ; বর্তমান ফল; সময় এখনও শেষ হয়নি। yesterday/ago দিয়ে ব্যবহার নিষেধ।

Present Perfect Continuous

has/have been + V-ing

অতীতে শুরু হয়ে এখনও চলছে। for = period, since = point.

Past Tenses

চারটি রূপ • সম্পূর্ণ নিয়ম ও উদাহরণ

Past Indefinite

I wrote • He wrote • did + V1

অতীতের একক কাজ, অভ্যাস, বা বিনয়সূচক ব্যবহার।

Past Continuous

was/were + V-ing

অতীতের কোনো মুহূর্তে চলমান কাজ।

Past Perfect

had + V3

অন্য অতীত কাজের আগে সম্পন্ন। “Before before, after after”

Past Perfect Continuous

had been + V-ing

অতীতের কোনো সময় পর্যন্ত চলমান কাজ।

Future Tenses

চারটি রূপ • সম্পূর্ণ নিয়ম ও উদাহরণ

Future Indefinite

shall/will + V1

ভবিষ্যতে ঘটবে এমন কাজ।

Future Continuous

shall/will + be + V-ing

ভবিষ্যতের কোনো সময়ে চলমান কাজ।

Future Perfect

shall/will + have + V3

ভবিষ্যতের কোনো সময়ের আগে সম্পন্ন কাজ। Modern usage often prefers Simple Future.

Future Perfect Continuous

No longer in practical use and therefore not treated.

Strong & Weak Verbs

Art. 185–189 • সম্পূর্ণ শ্রেণিবিভাগ

185. Two Classes of Verbs

Strong Verbs form Past Tense by changing an inside vowel (never by adding suffix). Past Participle may end in -en/-n.

কেবল ভিতরের কোন vowel পরিবর্তন করিয়া যে সকল verbs-এর Past Tense হয় তাহারা strong verbs।

sing–sang–sung • drive–drove–driven • write–wrote–written • take–took–taken • go–went–gone

Weak Verbs form Past Tense by adding -d or -t (with or without vowel change).

যে সকল verb-এর শেষে d, t যোগ করিয়া Past Tense হয়, তাহারা weak verbs।

leap–leaped • dream–dreamt • work–worked • bend–bent • bleed–bled

Note: Formerly called Irregular (Strong) and Regular (Weak). Some weak verbs change vowel but still add -d/-t (teach–taught, seek–sought).

186. Special Weak Verbs

  • Same form in three tenses: cut, cost, hit, let, put, shut…
  • Change -d → -t: bend–bent, build–built, lend–lent, send–sent, spend–spent
  • Only shorten the vowel: bleed–bled, feed–fed, meet–met

Decisive mark of Strong conjugation = absence of -d/-t suffix in Past Tense.

189. Zero-change Verbs

bet, burst, cast, cost, cut, hit, hurt, let, put, rid, set, shed, shut, split, spread, thrust

Important Notes on Past Participles

Italicised forms are mainly used as adjectives, not as parts of verbs:

bended knees, bounden duty, drunken man, ill-gotten money, hidden motive, graven image, molten lead, shrunken limb, sunken eyes, famine-stricken people.

Modern preference: burnt, dreamt, dwelt, knelt, leapt, smelt, spelt (instead of burned, dreamed…).

Confusing Verbs

Art. 188 • বিভ্রান্তিকর ক্রিয়া – সম্পূর্ণ নিয়ম ও উদাহরণ

Lie / Lay

Lie (rest – থাকা) intransitive → lie – lay – lain
It lay / has lain here. He has lain in bed.

Lie (false – মিথ্যা বলা) intransitive → lie – lied – lied
He lies. He has lied to his father.

Lay (put – রাখা) transitive → lay – laid – laid
Lay it here. He has laid the foundation. The hen has laid eggs.

⚠️ laid belongs to lay (রাখা); lain belongs to lie (থাকা). Lay is also the past of lie (থাকা).

Hang

Suspend (ঝুলান) → hung – hung  |  Execute (ফাঁসি) → hanged – hanged

He hung the coat on the peg. The murderer was hanged.

Fly / Flee / Flow

fly → flew – flown (ওড়া)

flee → fled – fled (পলায়ন করা)

flow → flowed – flowed (স্রোত বহা)

⚠️ The river has overflowed (not overflown). The bird has flown (not fled). The man has fled (not flown).

Born / Borne

bear (give birth – প্রসব করা) → born  |  bear (carry – বহন করা) → borne

He was born of poor parents. She has borne two sons. He was borne on the shoulder.

Strike / Stroke

strike → struck / stricken  |  stroke → stroked

He struck me. The people were awe-stricken / famine-stricken. She stroked the cat.

stroke is also the noun form of strike, never its past tense.

Lose / Loose / Loss

lose (verb) – cease to have: I do not want to lose the money.
loose (adj./verb) – free / make free: The box was tied loose. Wine loosed his tongue.
loss (noun) – that which is lost: He suffered a great loss.

Awake / Wake

awake → awoke (rarely awaked) – awaked (rarely awoke)
wake → woke / waked – waked (rarely woke / waken)

Both forms are accepted in modern English.

Await / Wait

Await is always transitive. Wait is not always intransitive.

I await your decision. I wait for your decision. (Both correct)

⚠️ “I am awaiting to hear…” is not English.
